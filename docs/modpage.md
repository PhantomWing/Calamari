# Calamari

Squid have always been a familiar sight drifting through Minecraft's oceans. This mod gives them a little more to offer: squids and glow squids now drop Calamari! Cook it over a campfire, sell it to the village fisherman, share it with your cat, or feed it to a dolphin to be led to buried treasure.

## 🎣 Where to find it

- Squid and glow squid drop 1–2 raw calamari.
- Occasionally pulled up while fishing.
- Dropped by guardians and elder guardians, and now and then by dolphins.
- Found in village fisher chests and buried treasure, and turns up in trial chamber rewards.
- A cat may leave some as a morning gift; a Hero of the Village fisherman may gift some too.

<br>
<br>

## 🧩 How it fits in

- Cats and ocelots take calamari as food.
- Wolves eat it raw or cooked.
- Give it to a dolphin and they lead you to treasure!
- Fisherman villagers buy raw calamari and sell the cooked version.
- Tagged as seafood (`c:foods`, `c:foods/raw_fish`, `c:foods/cooked_fish`, `c:foods/seafood`) to ensure compatibility with other mods.
